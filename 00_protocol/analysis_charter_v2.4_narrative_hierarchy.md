## Primary evidence hierarchy

1. **Replicated positive increment:** M1 improves AUC beyond age and sex in all 4 complete-cohort holdouts (increments 0.0464-0.1122).
2. **Clinical tension:** improved ranking does not ensure accurate absolute risk; CHARLS is underpredicted by 8.9 percentage points and MHAS is overpredicted by 11.1 points, with worse M1 Brier score in MHAS.
3. **General validation contribution:** pooled random splits meet the aggregate calibration region in 100/100 repetitions while concealing at least 1 cohort-specific failure in 99/100.
4. **Practical increment:** under primary equal-cohort development weighting, intercept updating adds 18.8 and 14.3 net true positives per 1000 at the illustrative 20% threshold in CHARLS and MHAS, compared with 1.1 and -0.3 in ELSA and HRS.
5. **Robustness and exploration:** outcome, weighting, follow-up, temporal, history-model, and HRS person-equal analyses remain secondary. The post-result HRS directional test stays fully disclosed in Methods and Supplement but is not a headline.

## Writing rules

- Title, Key Points, abstract, Results, Discussion, cover letter, and figures must present the 4 primary layers in the same order.
- Use the exact outcome term **new functional difficulty**; do not substitute dependence, inability, or need for help.
- Report net-benefit increments as net true positives per 1000 or net true-positive equivalents per 1000; do not describe them as literal additional patients correctly identified.
- Do not claim a universal updating trigger, minimum recalibration sample size, country effect, prospective impact, or deployment readiness.
- End on the positive evaluation pathway supported by the data, with limitations stated once in their proper section.

## Journal-format decision

The study remains an Original Investigation classified as a **Cohort Study**. JAMA Network Open currently defines Diagnostic/Prognostic Study as prospective, whereas this analysis is retrospective. STROBE therefore remains the design-level reporting framework, with TRIPOD+AI and TRIPOD-Cluster explicitly applied to prediction-model methods and results. Design, Setting, and Participants remain separate abstract headings for submission, as the journal instructs.

## Stop rule

After narrative, figure, document-render, vector, metadata, placeholder, and release-integrity gates pass, v2.4 is final. Remaining author identities, institutional determination, disclosures, funding, exact AI product/model, and repository DOI are author-controlled submission actions, not grounds for another scientific revision cycle.
